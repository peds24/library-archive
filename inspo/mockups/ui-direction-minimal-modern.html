What changed vs. current build

Accent moves off amber (currently doubling as both brand color and the TBR status chip) to a dedicated indigo, so status color always means status and nothing else. Card radius tightens from 16–24px to 10–14px for a less "cozy," more precise feel.

Open question

Covers here are generated placeholders (flat tint + title) since real Open Library covers arrive inconsistently sized. Worth deciding: keep real cover art as primary with this as the empty-state fallback, or commit to generated covers everywhere for visual consistency?
